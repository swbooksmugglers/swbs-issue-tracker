# My Wishlist

My Wishlist is your personal list of books from the catalog that you want to acquire but don't own yet. Think of it as a personal want list or "in search of" list.

Things to know:
* You can only have one entry per book — no duplicates.
* You can't wishlist a book you already own in your collection.
* Your wishlist is private — only you can see it.

## Removing from My Wishlist

There are a couple of ways a book can be removed from the wishlist.
* adding a book to your collection will automatically remove it from your wishlist
* you can also use the "Remove from Wishlist" option from the [Action Menu](../common/actionmenu.md) when you're on the My Wishlist page
