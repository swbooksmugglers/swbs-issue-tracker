# My Collection

My Collection is the user's personal library manager for their owned Star Wars books. You will find your collection listed and have the ability to add personal details to your copy of the books.

## Barcode Scanner

<img width="151" height="70" alt="scan-button" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ca09547-00c3-40b8-ac3f-e4a842425920" />

The `Scan` button allows you to use your camera or web cam as a barcode scanner. A successful scan will search the catalog using the ISBN from the barcode and add the title to your collection.

You'll be prompted to allow the scanner to have permissions to your camera.

<img width="613" height="621" alt="scanner-modal" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ed10289-959d-4877-beb7-89a58a5765e8" />

The `Quick Add` toggle allows you to skip the `Edit My Book` popup when adding to your collection. This allows you to quickly scan multiple books without being prompted to enter your book's details on each scan.


## Edit My Book

<img width="311" height="255" alt="action-menu" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ac7092e6-c576-469a-9e33-86fbefa42988" />

The [Edit My Book](../common/editbook.md) option from the [Action Menu](../common/actionmenu.md) allows you to annotate your books with personal details.

## Remove from My Collection

To remove a book from your collection simply select the "Remove from My Collection" option from the [Action Menu](../common/actionmenu.md).
