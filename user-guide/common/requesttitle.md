# Request Title

<img width="192" height="62" alt="request-title" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b5f61184-6d46-4d79-b81e-0274cbc5afe6" />

If a title is missing from the database, you can use the `Reques Title` feature to get the missing title added. The `Request Title` button can be found on the Catalog page and on the [global search results](search.md#header-global-search).

<img width="631" height="576" alt="request-title-form" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/287aeae5-0e6a-474c-abba-8f98e1de0881" />

Clicking on the `Request Title` button will open a form, fill out the details on the missing title and submit the form. The more details you can provide on the missing title, will make it easier for the admin team to find it and import it.

## Requested Title Updates

Your requested titles will automatically show up in the catalog within 24-48 hours of your request.

The Home page's [Requested Title Updates](../home/home.md#requested-title-updates) section will display the most recent added titles to the catalog.
