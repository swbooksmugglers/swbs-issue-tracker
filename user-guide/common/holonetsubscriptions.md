# Holonet Subscriptions

The Holonet includes a subscription feature that let's you get notifications based on your subscription preferences.

You can subscribe to all new Holonet updates or subscribe to specific a specific author's events.

## Subscription Preferences

<img width="245" height="76" alt="0-subscriptions-button" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0b0e23dd-0e68-46a3-8612-502fd08d2847" />

The Holonet has a `Subscription` button that will allow you to configure your subscription preferences.

<img width="654" height="347" alt="1-subscription-management" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5d4669db-62d5-4e82-83e7-15fd59a107a9" />

You can choose to be notified if any new event is added to the Holonet, or you can choose to be notified about events that a specific author is attending.

## Subscription Notifications

<img width="1130" height="422" alt="2-home-page-with-notifications" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2136ad60-9c55-44d3-a20b-916df1e036e3" />

When a Holonet event is added that meets your subscription preferences, you'll see a badge on your notifications icon and also under the Holonet Updates section on your Home page.

<img width="398" height="165" alt="3-notification" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/14d31459-61d4-4c41-89c7-513b9da1c234" />

Clicking on the notification will display the notification panel.

<img width="1140" height="258" alt="4-holonet-updates" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f31f34fb-298a-4115-87a8-76fe106161ba" />

The Holonet Updates will display a summary of your subscribed events. Clicking the `Mark As Read` will clear the Holonet Updates.

## Adding Holonet Update to My Events

<img width="201" height="118" alt="5-add-to-event" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7bb55c62-b986-4e9e-898c-51ed56838989" />
